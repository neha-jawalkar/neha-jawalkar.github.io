Hi! I'm Neha Jawalkar, a sixth (final) year PhD student at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in) in Bangalore, India. I'm advised by Dr. Arkaprava Basu, and I'm a part of the Computer Systems Lab. My research focuses on building large-scale, efficient, and cryptographically-secure systems for machine learning. I've been fortunate enough to work on cryptography with Dr. Chaya Ganesh, and the EzPC group at Microsoft Research.
