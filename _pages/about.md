Hi! I'm Neha Jawalkar, a sixth (final) year PhD student at the [Indian Institute of Science](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in) in Bangalore, India. I'm advised by [Dr. Arkaprava Basu](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~arkapravab/), and I'm a part of the [Computer Systems Lab](https://csl.csa.iisc.ac.in). My research focuses on building large-scale, efficient and *cryptographically-secure* systems for machine learning. I've been fortunate enough to work with [Dr. Chaya Ganesh](https://www.csa.iisc.ac.in/~chaya/), and the [EzPC group](https://www.microsoft.com/en-us/research/project/ezpc-easy-secure-multi-party-computation/) at Microsoft Research.
